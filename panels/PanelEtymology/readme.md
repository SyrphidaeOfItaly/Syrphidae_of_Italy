# PanelEtymology (`panel:etymology`)

Overview tab, genus and species pages. Title "Name: gender, form and etymology".

- The **name** itself, large (the epithet for species, the whole name for genera), with **pills**
  for its grammar, from the name's "Latinized" classifications: gender (masculine, feminine,
  neuter) and part of speech (adjective, participle, noun in apposition, noun in genitive case).
- One **line explaining what it means**: nouns keep their ending whatever the genus;
  adjectives and participles agree with the genus; a genus's gender sets the endings of the
  adjectival species names in it.
- For adjectives and participles, the **masculine, feminine and neuter forms** entered on the name.
- The **etymology** text (Markdown) in a highlighted box.

English and Italian through `useT`. Data: the taxon of the page and
`/taxon_name_classifications?taxon_name_id[]=`. Hidden when there is nothing to show.
